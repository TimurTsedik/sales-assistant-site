The problem

You open the dialer with nothing, and the client expects answers

History is scattered

Dialer, recorder, WhatsApp, Telegram and your own memory. Assembling the picture in a minute before a call is impossible.

Promises get lost

“I’ll call you Thursday” lives in your head until the first busy day — then the client goes to whoever did call.

CRM misses it

Corporate CRM never sees calls from your personal mobile or chats in messengers. The real conversation stays outside the system.

Privacy

Privacy here is architecture, not a promise

No cloud

Data sits in the app’s private storage on your phone. We simply do not run a server.

No analytics

No trackers, no ad SDKs, no event collection. There is nobody to look at your data.

Internet once

Only to download the speech-recognition model. After that everything works offline.

Take it all as Markdown

Obsidian-format export and confirmable deletion, any time, with no support ticket.
